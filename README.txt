MY GYM PLAN PREMIUM V5

Premium five-tab navigation
- Home: today's workout, streak, monthly workouts, weight, top PR, recent volume and recent activity.
- Train: all routines, last-trained date, prior volume, duration, templates and routine editing.
- Progress: weight, weekly volume, 35-day heatmap, exercise progression, lifetime statistics and history.
- Records: ranked exercise PRs, estimated 1RM and achievement badges.
- Profile: account, sync, export, light/dark mode, body weight and rest-timer settings.

Workout experience
- Previous completed sets shown for every exercise.
- Workout summary after Finish with duration, sets, volume, exercises and new PRs.
- Premium bottom rest timer with +30 seconds and Skip.
- Timer stops on Finish or Sign out and never covers the Finish CTA.

Install
1. Replace index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png and icon-512.png in GitHub.
2. Keep the current Supabase project and data. No schema change is needed.
3. Open the deployed app with ?v=5 once. Remove and re-add the Home Screen app if it remains cached.
